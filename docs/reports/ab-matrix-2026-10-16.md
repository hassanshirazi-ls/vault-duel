# A/B balance matrix: R1 (sickness), R2 (lanes), R3 (element ring), 2026-10-16

Branch phase-1, HEAD 1605fb3 plus the uncommitted sim plumbing (`packages/sim`). No default, global or content value was changed; every cell is a command-line override. Per-cell JSON is in `docs/reports/ab/<cell>.json` (finals: `docs/reports/ab/full-<cell>.json`).

## How the cells were run

- `sim quick --games 2000 --seed-dates 10 --from 2026-10-16 --bots expert,greedy,novice --workers 10 --seed-check`, the same for every cell. Seed `--from` and `--seed-dates` are identical, so every bot in every cell plays the same 10 dates (200 rerolls each).
- `--seed-check` (new) runs the difficulty check on the same 10 dates. It is a small sample: the baseline reads 89.3% here and 87.6% on 60 dates.
- Overrides per cell: R1 = `--set SICKNESS_MODE=monstersOnly`; R2 = `--set FLAGS.LANES=true`; ring A = `--ring A` (ember,gale,tide,lumen,umbra); B = `--ring B`; C = `--ring C`; L = `--collector-reposition laneAware` (sets `collector.json › ai.reposition`). The 123-S0, -NS, -R2 and -G cells add `SENTINEL_REACH=0`, `FLAGS.LANE_SWAP=false`, `LANE_MOVE_RANGE=2` and `FLAGS.APPRAISED_ENTERS_GUARD=true` to 123L. The guard flag exists in `globals.ts`, so that cell was run.
- Noise: with 2000 games the 95% interval on a win rate is about plus or minus 2.2 points (greedy about 1 point). Differences under 3 points between cells are not reliable.
- Pass/fail marks follow the DESIGN targets read from the run's own globals: expert win 45-55%, median rounds 9-11, rounds 8-12 more than half, round 12 15-30%, grafts per win 1.5-3, Appraisal 30-60%, Unpriced 20-40%, novice 20-30%, seed rejection at most 15%. Greedy has no target; vault sealed, Unstable and damage have no target (information only). Cells ran on a shared machine, so duels/s/core is indicative only.

## 2. Reading each lever

Early-ending detail (quick, 2000 games per bot):

**R1 alone (monsters-only sickness)** shortens and tilts the game toward the player. Expert 46.6% to 57.4%, greedy 6.1% to 21.1%, median round 6 to 5, expert wins by round 4 nearly double (16.4% to 30.9%), damage per round 5.56 to 6.73. Unpriced appearance falls from 24.9% to 15.2% (fail) because the Collector's ace arrives after more games are over. It moves the round targets the wrong way (rounds 8-12 share 16.9% to 8.1%, round 12 share 2.0% to 0.3%). Vault-sealed endings nearly vanish for the expert (1.2% to 0.1%).

**R2 alone (lanes, Collector on the default `toAttackIfUsable`)** lengthens the game (mean rounds 5.96 to 7.00, rounds 8-12 share 16.9% to 37.6%, round 12 share 2.0% to 11.8%) and cuts damage per round (5.56 to 4.57). It is the only lever that moves the round-length targets toward their bands, but it also raises the expert's win rate (46.6% to 57.4%, fails the 45-55% band) and doubles-plus the share of games ending in the sealed Vault (expert 1.2% to 9.7%, novice 18.3% to 39.4%). Greedy is unchanged (6.1% to 5.7%).

**Ring alone (R3)** is a null result at this sample size. Expert win rate A 45.2%, B 44.1%, C 46.8% against 46.6% baseline; round shape, vault, appraisal, Unpriced, grafts per win are all within noise. The one visible effect is Unstable Chimeras per game, 1.10 to 1.24-1.25 for all three rings (the ring has 5 opposing pairs where the old matrix had 4). B is the lowest expert win rate (44.1%, just under the band) and A is nearest to baseline; the three are not separable by this matrix. Ring does matter in combination: cell 23 (R2 + ring A) lengthens games further than cell 2 (median 7 vs 6, rounds 8-12 40.7% vs 37.6%, round 12 13.8% vs 11.8%), but that gap is inside noise too, and on the 60-date full run the two are close (38.7% vs 37.9%, 13.2% vs 12.6%).

**What `laneAware` changes.** Under lanes (cell 2 to 2L) the Collector wins more: expert 57.4% to 51.2% (into the band), round 12 share 11.8% to 7.3%, vault-sealed 9.7% to 5.1%, damage to player per round 1.43 to 1.84. It makes games shorter and the Collector stronger, which is what a smarter Collector should do. In the combined package (12 to 12L, 123 to 123L) the effect is smaller: expert 67.7% to 65.0% and 68.0% to 65.9%, inside noise. With R1 on, the expert wins most games by round 4 regardless of reposition mode. `laneAware` is the only change that brings an R2 cell's expert win rate into the band, so it is a strong candidate for the default if R2 ships alone.

**The owner's worry: one side demolishing the other with fresh summons under R1.** The data says R1 does tilt it, toward the player. R1 alone: damage to the Collector per round rises from 3.32 to 4.40 (+33%) while damage to the player stays at 2.25 to 2.33; the expert wins by round 4 in 30.9% of games instead of 16.4%. Adding lanes (R1 to R1+R2) does not blunt it: damage per round goes 6.73 to 6.41 (about 5% lower overall), but that is because damage to the player falls (2.33 to 1.81) while damage to the Collector rises (4.40 to 4.60); expert wins by round 4 rise again to 37.8% and win rate to 67.7%, greedy to 28.9%. Under R1+R2 the median winning round is 4, same as R1. So lanes make the player side's fresh summons safer, not less dangerous. If the concern is a rush, R1+R2 is worse than R1 alone for the Collector. (For the greedy bot the same direction holds: damage to the Collector per round 2.54 under R1, 2.99 under R1+R2.) Whether the player bots overstate this against a human is outside what the sim measures.

**Lane sub-levers on 123L** (all inside noise of 123L, expert 65.9%): `SENTINEL_REACH=0` 66.5%, `LANE_SWAP=false` 65.7%, `LANE_MOVE_RANGE=2` 67.2%, `APPRAISED_ENTERS_GUARD` 69.0% (greedy 31.6%). None moves the round-length targets (median 5 everywhere, rounds 8-12 11.6-13.3%). None of them is a useful balance lever once R1 is on; `LANE_MOVE_RANGE=2` and the guard flag cost speed and are the slowest cells. Their seed-rejection and fallback counts (3-4 fallback dates of 10) are higher than 123L's (2), which is within the noise of a 10-date check.

## 4. Anomalies

- No crashes, no stuck games, no round-0 endings and no games past MAX_ROUNDS in any of the 17 quick cells or 4 full runs (all `crashed`, `stuck`, overflow-bin counts are 0).
- D-004 fallback dates (target: never) in every cell: 1-4 of 10 dates in quick (baseline 1), 1-5 of 60 in the finals. The baseline already has 4 of 60.
- Seed rejection at 83-95% in every cell (target at most 15%). Baseline itself is 87.6%. A rule change does not fix it.
- Novice win rate is exactly 0% in several cells (flagged by the sim as an anomaly, as in the baseline).
- Performance: duels/s/core is 96 for the baseline and 42-70 for lane cells (about 1.4-2.3 times slower; R2-only cells are the slowest, 42-49), against PERF_DUELS_PER_SEC_PER_CORE (1000). Already failing at baseline (139 on the committed full run; 77-96 here with other work running). The D-299 note (2.4 times slower under lanes) is consistent. Not a regression from this task; the cost of the new per-action counters was not isolated.
- Impossible values: none. Per-round damage and shares are all in range; vault-sealed share for the novice is 14-40%, high because the novice rarely finishes a game by combat.
- A single caveat on the cells: quick cells use 10 seed dates, so seed-rejection and fallback figures per cell are noisy; the finals' 60-date numbers are the ones to use.

## 5. Plumbing as built (`packages/sim`)

- `--ring A|B|C|a,b,c,d,e`: rewrites `elements.json` `beats` into a single cycle on a clone of the raw content, before `parseContent` and `compileContent` (new `src/overrides.ts`, called from `world.ts › loadContext`). Rejects anything that is not a permutation of the element ids.
- `--collector ai.<path>=<json>` (repeatable; a non-JSON value is taken as a bare word) and the shorthand `--collector-reposition MODE`. Paths must begin with `ai.`. The schema still validates the result.
- Workers receive the same strings in the `init` message and call the same `loadContext`, so compiled content agrees. The content hash in the report reflects the overrides; the report header now lists `--set` overrides, content overrides and the globals hash (new `config.contentOverrides`, `config.globalsHash` in the JSON).
- `sim quick --seed-check` runs the difficulty check as well, so a quick cell has a seed-rejection metric.
- New per-bot metrics in the report and aggregate (existing definitions unchanged): `vaultSealedShare`, `medianWinRound`, `unstablePerGame`, `unstableGameShare`, `damagePerRound`, `damageToCollectorPerRound`, `damageToPlayerPerRound`, `directDamageShare`. Damage is the change in life across each action, summed per game and divided by the rounds played; direct damage is life lost on an `attack` with target -1. Unstable counts a `graftPosition` action that adds a player monster with `unstableUntil >= 0` entering this turn.
- Tests: `packages/sim/test/overrides.test.ts` (parse, apply, hash change, default path identical, workers 1 vs 2 agree and differ from default) and two cases in `report.test.ts`.
- Check: `sim quick --games 400 --seed-dates 4 --from 2026-11-01` with no overrides gives a `results` object with zero differences in every pre-existing field against `simA/quick-2026-10-16.json`; the only additions are the eight new bot fields.
